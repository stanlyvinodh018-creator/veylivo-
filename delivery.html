Digital Product Delivery

Last updated: September 1, 2026

How delivery works

Veylivo products are digital. After successful payment, the purchased product is delivered electronically through the checkout/store platform used for the order.

Access

Delivery instructions or download access are provided after payment confirmation. Delivery timing can depend on the payment provider and the store platform.

Problems with delivery

If you complete a purchase but cannot access your digital product, contact Veylivo using the email shown on our Contact page with your order details so we can assist.

Physical shipping

Veylivo's products are digital unless a product listing specifically states otherwise. No physical shipment is made for a digital-only product.
